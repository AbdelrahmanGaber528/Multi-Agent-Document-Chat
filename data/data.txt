# Atlas Cloud Systems — Q3 2026 Financial & Operational Report
**Document ID:** FIN-2026-Q3  
**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026  

## 1. Executive Summary
In Q3 2026, Atlas Cloud Systems generated total revenue of $48.6 million, up from $41.2 million in Q2 2026. Operating expenses were $31.4 million, resulting in an operating income of $17.2 million. Customer churn dropped to 1.8%, down from 2.4% in Q2 2026.

## 3. Infrastructure & SLA Performance
Atlas maintained an average platform uptime of 99.97% across all production regions during Q3 2026. Mean API response latency was 118 ms, and total engineering headcount reached 240 full-time employees at quarter close.
