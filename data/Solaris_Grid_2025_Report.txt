1. Facility Profile & Commercial Terms

Its primary storage technology is Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP), with a deployed storage capacity of 420 MWh, a round-trip efficiency of 89.5%, and an annual maintenance cost of $3.4 million. In 2025, Solaris generated $64.0 million in total revenue against $46.8 million in operating expenses.
