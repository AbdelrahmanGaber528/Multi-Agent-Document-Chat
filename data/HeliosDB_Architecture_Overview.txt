HeliosDB Enterprise — Technical Architecture & Pricing Overview

1. Storage & Replication Engine
HeliosDB is a distributed transactional database built on a Log-Structured Merge-tree (LSM) storage engine. For cluster coordination and fault tolerance, HeliosDB uses the Raft consensus protocol across availability zones.

2. Security & Reliability Benchmarks
All customer volumes in HeliosDB are encrypted at rest using AES-256. In production benchmarks, HeliosDB guarantees an SLA uptime of 99.99% and achieves a cold-start failover recovery time of 18 seconds.

3. Commercial Licensing
The HeliosDB Enterprise starter cluster is priced at a monthly base price of $450.
