VortexDB Cloud — Technical Architecture & Pricing Overview

1. Storage & Replication Engine
VortexDB is a cloud-native operational database powered by a Log-Structured Merge-tree (LSM) storage engine. High availability and leader election are managed via the Raft consensus protocol.

2. Security & Reliability Benchmarks
VortexDB enforces AES-256 encryption at rest by default across all storage nodes. The platform provides a guaranteed SLA uptime of 99.95% for multi-region deployments.

3. Commercial Licensing
The VortexDB Cloud production tier starts at a monthly base price of $620.
